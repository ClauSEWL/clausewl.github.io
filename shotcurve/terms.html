Not medical advice

Shotcurve is a personal log and does not provide medical advice or dosing. It has no dose calculator and no unit conversion, and it never recommends a medication, an amount or a schedule. Level curves, peaks, troughs and forecasts are mathematical estimates from the information you enter and published half-lives; they are not lab values and must not be used to make treatment decisions. Always follow your prescriber. Shotcurve is not a medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
