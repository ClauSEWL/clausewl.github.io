Shotcurve

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: we collect no data. Shotcurve has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your log lives on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database, where only you can reach it. Apple Health data is never copied to iCloud.

Shotcurve is an injection and medication log made by Clau SEWL (“we”). This policy explains what the app stores, where, and why. It applies to the Shotcurve app for iPhone and its widgets.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data, health data, usage data, identifiers or diagnostics. The app contains no third-party SDKs. Its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Your log: on your device and in your private iCloud

What you enter (medications, schedules, doses, injection sites, side effects, check-ins, weights, lab results and notes) is stored in the app on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for Shotcurve, Apple keeps a copy in the private database of your iCloud account so your devices stay in sync. That copy is stored by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we cannot see or access it. If iCloud is off or unavailable, everything stays on the device only.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Shotcurve reads your body weight, and the protein, fiber and water you logged in other apps, from Apple Health to show them next to your own entries, and saves the weights you log in Shotcurve to Apple Health. Data read from Apple Health is shown in the app and is not saved to the iCloud-synced log, not sent anywhere, not used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Face ID

The optional Face ID lock uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework. Your face data is handled entirely by iOS; Shotcurve only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never sees or stores biometric data.

Reminders, alarms, widgets and Siri

Shot reminders, alarms, refill reminders and the free-trial reminder are scheduled and delivered locally on your iPhone. Widgets, Live Activities and Siri / Shortcuts answers are computed on your device from a small summary that the app shares with its own widgets through an App Group on the device. None of this leaves your device.

Weekly summary

The optional weekly summary is written on your device by Apple’s on-device language model from statistics of your own log. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Purchases

Shotcurve Pro (yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple gives developers aggregate, non-identifying sales reports through App Store Connect.

Import, export and backup

CSV, JSON and PDF files (exports, backups and the doctor visit summary) are created on your device and leave it only when you choose where to save or share them. A CSV file you import from another app or a spreadsheet is read on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Network

The app connects only to Apple: iCloud for your private sync, and the App Store for purchases. It does not contact any server run by us or by anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can edit or delete any entry in the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. To remove the synced copy, delete Shotcurve’s data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or delete the entries in the app while iCloud is on.

Not medical advice

Shotcurve is a personal log and does not provide medical advice or dosing. Level curves are estimates, not lab values. Always follow your prescriber.

Children

Shotcurve is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
