How do I log a shot?

On Today, tap Log shot. The amount and time come from your schedule, and the most rested injection site is highlighted as Suggested. Tap any other site (or No site) and that is exactly what gets saved. To log a shot you took earlier, change the date and time before saving. Every entry can be edited or deleted later from Progress → History. For tablets the button says Log dose and there is no site to pick.

What is the level curve? Why does it say “Estimated”?

The curve estimates how much medication is in your body over time, using your own dose history and a published half-life for the medication (or the one you enter for a custom medication). It is a model, not a lab value, and not medical advice. Tap “About this estimate” on any chart for the details. If you add lab results (Pro), you can show them on the chart for comparison; that is a visual aid only.

Why is there no dose calculator or unit conversion?

On purpose. Shotcurve is a personal log and does not provide medical advice or dosing. It stores amounts exactly as you type them and never suggests what or how much to take. Your prescriber and pharmacist decide that.

I missed a dose. What should I do?

Follow your prescriber’s instructions. In the app you can log the dose late (with its real time) or mark it as skipped; your schedule and reminders update either way.

Coming from another app?

Export your history as CSV from the other app (or a spreadsheet), then in Shotcurve choose Import CSV (in onboarding or in Settings). Match the columns (date, medication, amount, unit, site, notes), check the preview and import. Duplicates are skipped, and everything happens on your iPhone. Import is free.

My medication or schedule isn’t listed

Choose Custom when adding a medication: any name, any route, and optionally its half-life and time to peak for the curve. Schedules can be once or twice a week on the days you pick, every N days (including 3.5), daily up to four times, or as needed, counted from your last dose or on fixed days. Amounts are saved exactly as you type them. Titration steps can be added “as prescribed”.

Reminders and the alarm

Shotcurve schedules a notification for each planned dose and can also ring an alarm at shot time (it rings even in silent mode). Today shows when the next reminder is due, or “Reminders are off” with a button to turn them on. If reminders don’t arrive, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Shotcurve, and Settings → Shotcurve for alarms. Reminders are free.

Widgets

The next-shot widget (Home Screen and Lock Screen) and the Log shot control are free: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and search for Shotcurve. The level-curve widget and the Live Activity on shot day are part of Shotcurve Pro.

A report for my doctor

In Progress, tap Report to make a visit summary PDF for any date range: your doses, schedule, weight, side effects and notes. It is free. With Pro, it can also include the estimated-level charts and your lab results.

Sync between my devices

Sync is optional and off by default. Turn it on in Settings → Backup → iCloud sync on each device: Shotcurve then syncs what you type into it through your own private iCloud database (Apple Health data is never synced). Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on each device and that iCloud is allowed for Shotcurve (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud). Sync can take a few minutes on a new device. Without sync, your data still moves to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup, or use Settings → Backup.

Backup, restore and export

Settings → Backup creates a JSON backup and lets you choose where to save it: Files, iCloud Drive or anywhere else. Save it outside Shotcurve, because deleting the app deletes the app’s own files. Settings shows the date of your last backup, and you can restore a backup at any time. You can also export your history (with notes, sites and skipped doses) as CSV. Backup, restore, import and export are free, always.

Apple Health

Turn it on in Settings → Apple Health. Shotcurve reads your weight, and the protein, fiber and water you logged in other apps, to show them next to your own entries, and can save the weights you log. Data read from Apple Health is shown live and is never copied into the iCloud-synced log.

Face ID lock

Settings → Face ID lock asks for Face ID (or your passcode) when the app opens.

Shotcurve Pro, trials and restoring purchases

Pro adds the full level history with a 14-day forecast, lab results on the chart, unlimited medications, side-effect patterns and a weekly summary, the level-curve widget and Live Activity, level charts and labs in the doctor PDF, refill forecasts and app icons. Anything that is free stays free; Pro only adds. It is available yearly (with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, at the same yearly price), monthly, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. If you start the trial, Shotcurve reminds you two days before it ends.

Bought Pro on another device? Tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the Pro screen, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription or ask for a refund

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Shotcurve Pro, or see Apple’s guide to cancelling. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period (or of the trial) to avoid the next charge. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
