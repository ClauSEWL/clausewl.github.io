Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Reelcast is built to be private by design. We have no user accounts, we run no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not collect or sell your personal data. This policy explains the little data the app does use, and why.

Location

If you grant location access, Reelcast uses your location to compute the solunar feeding windows for your spot and to request the local weather and barometric-pressure forecast. Your location is used on your device for these calculations. It is not stored on our servers (we have none) and is never sold or shared for advertising. The core solunar forecast also works offline.

Weather data

Weather and pressure forecasts are provided by Apple Weather (WeatherKit). To return a forecast, the app sends the relevant coordinates to Apple. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Saved spots & catch log

Spots you save and the catches you log (including any photos and the captured conditions) are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

Reelcast Pro is available as an auto-renewable subscription (weekly or yearly) or as a one-time lifetime purchase, all handled entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple may share aggregate, non-identifying purchase information with us through App Store Connect.

Notifications

If you enable bite-window alerts, reminders are scheduled and delivered locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Children

Reelcast is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
